Factory Flow to Fortune - Assembly Line Optimization

An interactive visualization system for optimizing refrigerator assembly line operations. This tool helps analyze production efficiency, identify bottlenecks, and maximize profitability through data-driven assembly line configuration.

Project Objectives

Design and optimize assembly line configurations to achieve maximum efficiency and profitability:

  • Throughput Optimization – Maximize units produced per hour
  • Bottleneck Analysis – Identify and resolve production constraints
  • Labor Efficiency – Balance workstation assignments across employees
  • Cost Minimization – Reduce idle time and operational costs
  • Quality Assurance – Maintain proper assembly sequence precedence

Manufacturing Data

The system processes real assembly line data including:

  • Product Mix – Super (60%), Ultra (25%), Mega (15%) refrigerator models
  • Assembly Elements – 50+ individual manufacturing tasks with time requirements
  • Precedence Constraints – Required order dependencies between assembly steps
  • Workstation Capacity – Variable employee assignments (3-13 workers)
  • Financial Parameters – Labor costs, material costs, and profit margins

Key Performance Indicators

Real-time metrics calculated from your current configuration:

  • Production Rate – Current throughput and capacity utilization
  • Cycle Time – Time between completed units
  • Line Balance – Workstation efficiency distribution
  • Idle Time Analysis – Identify underutilized resources
  • Daily Gross Profit – Financial performance projections

Visualization Dashboard

Interactive tabs provide comprehensive analysis views:

  • Precedence Network – Visualize assembly step dependencies and constraints
  • Production Schedule – Real-time Gantt chart showing workstation timelines
  • Efficiency Analysis – Compare individual vs. line-wide performance metrics
  • Factory Layout – Animated U-shaped assembly line with product flow
  • Profit Optimization – Demand vs. profitability analysis and forecasting

Interactive Controls

Modify parameters to test different scenarios:

  • Demand Settings – Adjust daily production targets
  • Staffing Levels – Change number of assembly workers
  • Operating Hours – Modify shift length and scheduling
  • Task Assignment – Drag and drop elements between workstations
  • Cost Parameters – Update labor rates and material costs

Getting Started Guide

Follow these steps to optimize your assembly line:

1
Set Parameters – Adjust demand, staffing, and operational settings in the right sidebar
2
Arrange Tasks – Drag assembly elements between workstations to balance the line
3
Analyze Results – Monitor efficiency metrics and identify bottlenecks
4
Explore Views – Switch between tabs to visualize different aspects of your line

Optimization Tips

Key strategies for achieving optimal assembly line performance:

💡
Balance Workload – Aim for similar cycle times across all workstations to minimize idle time
⚡
Respect Precedence – Ensure assembly steps follow the required order dependencies
📊
Monitor Efficiency – Target 85%+ line efficiency for optimal performance
🎯
Meet Demand – Ensure throughput meets or exceeds daily production targets
